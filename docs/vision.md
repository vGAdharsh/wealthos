# WealthOS Vision

## Mission

To help Indian salaried professionals understand, predict, and improve their financial future through AI-powered insights and decision support.

---

## Vision

Transform personal financial data into actionable insights, forecasts, and decisions.

---

## Long-Term Goal

WealthOS aims to evolve from:

Expense Tracker
↓
Financial Dashboard
↓
Net Worth Tracker
↓
Financial Intelligence System
↓
Decision Support System
↓
AI Wealth Copilot

---

## Target Users

- Indian salaried professionals
- Early investors
- MBA students
- Young professionals
- Wealth builders

---

## Core Philosophy

Data

Insights

Better Decisions
