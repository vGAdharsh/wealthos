# Problem Statement

## Background

Most Indian salaried professionals manage their finances across multiple platforms:

- Bank accounts
- UPI applications
- Mutual fund platforms
- Fixed deposits
- Provident Fund
- Insurance products

Although these platforms provide transaction histories and balances, they do not provide a unified view of financial health.

---

## Existing Challenges

### Fragmented Financial Information

Financial data is spread across multiple applications and institutions.

### Lack of Long-Term Visibility

Users know their current balances but cannot easily track:

- Net worth growth
- Savings trends
- Investment discipline

### Limited Decision Support

Most applications answer:

"What happened?"

They rarely answer:

- Why did it happen?
- What will happen?
- What should I do next?

### Absence of Financial Health Indicators

Users lack a simple metric to evaluate their overall financial wellness.

---

## Proposed Solution

WealthOS aims to provide:

- Unified financial visibility
- Net worth tracking
- Financial health scoring
- Wealth forecasting
- AI-powered insights
- Decision support

---

## Goal

Transform financial data into:

Data
↓
Insights
↓
Recommendations
↓
Better Decisions
↓
Wealth Creation
